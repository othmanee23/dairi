# DAIRI client decisions

This list contains business approvals only. Proposed defaults are recommendations, not approved policy or legal language. The client or their qualified adviser must supply/approve final wording and rules.

| Decision needed | Impact | Recommended default | Owner / evidence to close |
|---|---|---|---|
| Approve privacy policy and customer-data retention wording. | Registration and support pages cannot tell customers how their information is used, shared, or retained. | Client supplies legally reviewed text describing the actual data flows and retention; publish it on a dedicated Privacy page after approval. | DAIRI decision maker/legal adviser; approved copy and publication approval. |
| Approve Terms of Service and returns/exchanges/refunds policy. | Signup consent and customer expectations are incomplete; cancellation/payment steps are currently unpaid order requests. | Client/legal adviser supplies separate approved terms and returns/refund text; state clearly that checkout submits an unpaid order request and collects no payment. | DAIRI decision maker/legal adviser; approved copy and consent wording. |
| Confirm shipping and delivery rules, destinations, carriers, fees, and manual quote wording. | Checkout currently uses manual estimates or a studio quote; carrier rating, labels, and international calculations are not implemented. | For UAT, approve a small destination/rate example matrix and use a clearly labeled studio-confirmed estimate. Decide separately whether carrier-rate integration is required before production. | DAIRI fulfillment owner; signed-off destination matrix and manual-quote decision. |
| Approve tax jurisdictions, rates, taxable shipping, exemptions, and rounding examples with an accountant. | Current tax is manually configured; incorrect values can misstate customer totals and obligations. | Keep production tax collection disabled or clearly quote an estimate until the client/accountant approves a jurisdiction matrix. | DAIRI and tax adviser; approved matrix and checkout examples. |
| Decide discount behavior when an unpaid order is cancelled, including capped redemption codes. | Current cancellation restores inventory but leaves the code redemption consumed. | Release a redemption only after a cancellation is confirmed if the client wants cancelled unpaid orders to return capacity; this requires transactional implementation and tests. | DAIRI commerce owner; written rule for first/repeated cancellation. |
| Approve customer-facing order confirmation and shipment email copy. | The outbox templates make statements about unpaid status, order review, totals, and shipment; wording must match actual operations. | Keep the explicit “unpaid / no payment collected” message and send only after the verified staging sender and recipient controls are ready. | DAIRI marketing/operations; approved copy and sender identity. |
| Confirm guest-order access after the one-hour confirmation summary expires. | Guest orders are not shown in a signed-in account unless linked to that customer; the temporary browser summary is intentionally short-lived and PII-free. | Keep the order confirmation number available by email once email delivery is configured; require the guest to contact support for later assistance unless the client wants an account-linking flow. | DAIRI support owner; accepted guest order-retrieval behavior. |
| Approve wholesale eligibility, requested business details, pricing visibility, and minimum order quantity. | These choices determine who receives wholesale access and the terms shown to approved accounts. | Require manual approval and expose wholesale prices only after approval; use the client-approved MOQ and application fields. | DAIRI wholesale owner; eligibility checklist, fields, and MOQ. |
| Supply and approve the final product catalog and photography. | Demo products, prices, SKUs, stock, and synthetic test imagery are not suitable for client acceptance or customers. | Load only client-approved style data and images into isolated staging; do not copy production/customer records. | DAIRI catalog owner; approved products/prices/SKUs/inventory/photography. |
| Confirm contact details printed in the DAIRI template PDF. | The template currently embeds a website, mailing address, phone, and fax that may be stale or placeholders. | Confirm each detail or provide replacements before any PDF is shared externally. | DAIRI brand owner; written confirmation of contact details. |
| Approve remaining public business content, including accessibility/contact wording and returns/customer support instructions. | Generic support destinations do not satisfy final customer-facing information requirements. | Client provides the actual contact channel, service expectations, and accessibility statement/requirements; do not publish placeholder assurances. | DAIRI operations/legal; approved content and response expectations. |
